# Quran Tarteel Product Plan

## MVP 0.1 (included)
- Original Flutter UI
- Surah/Ayah demo reader
- Recording flow
- Mock AI word feedback
- Progress, Prayer, Qibla placeholders
- FastAPI service contract

## MVP 0.2
- Full licensed Quran dataset
- Surah/Juz/search/bookmarks
- Reciter streaming + offline cache
- Real prayer calculations and Qibla
- User-selected English/Bangla/Urdu translation packs

## AI 0.3
- Ayah-constrained Arabic/Quranic ASR
- Forced alignment
- Missing/repeated/inserted word detection
- Per-word confidence calibration

## AI 0.4
- Phoneme/makhraj scoring
- Tajweed rule features
- Teacher-reviewed feedback templates
- Benchmark set and error analysis

## Release readiness
- Privacy policy
- Audio retention controls
- Child/privacy review
- Content/audio/font licensing review
- iOS/Android permissions
- Accessibility and RTL QA
- Human validation of AI feedback
